How to Play

Goal: Score the most points by placing words on the board.

Starting: Each player receives 7 letter tiles from the shared pool (extra vowels included). The first word must cross the center ★ star.

Playing: Click a tile from your rack, then click a cell on the board to place it. Words must connect to existing letters — sharing at least one letter with a word already on the board.

Scoring: Each letter has a point value. ★ Double cells double a letter's value; ★★ Triple cells triple it.

Direction: Words can go across (→) or down (↓). All letters you place in one turn must be in the same row or column.

Recall: Use "Recall" to take back tiles you've placed this turn before confirming.

Skipping: You can skip your turn if you cannot form a word.

Game End: The game ends when the tile bag is empty and one player uses all their tiles, or both players skip consecutively. Highest score wins!
